## About us

MITF Dev Space is the **company-owned** GitHub organization for Masarat engineering. We are a Libya-based development department focused on reliable, secure software for regulated environments.

We work closely with product, operations, and compliance stakeholders to deliver systems that meet high standards for quality, availability, and data protection. Most of our repositories are **private** and used for internal development; only material that is approved for external sharing is published openly.

---

## Our work

We build and maintain full-stack digital platforms — backend services, web and mobile applications, APIs, infrastructure, and automation — across areas such as:

| Area | What we focus on |
|------|------------------|
| **Financial technology** | Digital wallets, payments, and banking experiences |
| **Compliance & risk** | Regulatory tooling, screening, and audit-friendly workflows |
| **Customer channels** | Web and mobile apps for end users and partners |
| **Operations** | Internal tools that help teams ship and support products safely |
| **Platform engineering** | Shared standards, CI/CD, observability, and developer experience |

We favor **clear ownership**, **sensible architecture**, and **practical delivery** over hype. Details of active products and client engagements stay inside the organization.

## Our team

Masarat Development is organized around how we actually build software:

| Practice | Focus |
|----------|--------|
| **Backend** | APIs, services, data, and integrations |
| **Web** | Portals, dashboards, and browser-based products |
| **Mobile** | iOS and Android applications |

## How we develop

Principles we apply across projects:

- **Security first** — least privilege, dependency monitoring, and responsible disclosure
- **Team-based access** — permissions aligned to role and need
- **Modern delivery** — automated builds, tests, and repeatable deployments
- **Maintainability** — readable code, documented decisions, and sustainable pace

Engineers with org access can find contributing guidelines and internal runbooks in this repository. If you are part of the team, start with [CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/mitf-dev-space/.github/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md).

---

## Joining the organization

Access is **by invitation only**. If you are a Masarat engineer:

1. Accept your GitHub organization invite
2. Confirm your team assignment with your lead
3. Read [CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/mitf-dev-space/.github/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md) before opening your first pull request

To report a security concern, follow [SECURITY.md](https://github.com/mitf-dev-space/.github/blob/main/SECURITY.md). Please do not disclose vulnerabilities in public issues.
